Roadmap

Scored against what the tree actually does, using the project's own three labels. A plan that does not distinguish shipped work from intention stops being useful about a week in.

How to read this

LabelMeans
DONEImplemented and verified, with the verification named.
PARTIALReal code exists and does something, with a stated gap.
OPENNot started — or a config key exists that is parsed and inert.
DISPUTEDThe project's own documents disagree, and both are deliberately kept.
BLOCKEDCannot proceed until something else lands.
A config key is not a feature. That is the project's own rule, and it is why ssao, shadows, per_pixel_lighting and muzzle_lights are scored OPEN despite being settable. Nothing here is promoted without a measurement.

Sources: docs/VISION.md (the long arc, with the numbered feature table this page follows), docs/ROADMAP.md (what is being worked on now), and the repository's open issues and milestones. Reviewed by hand on 2026-09-09.

Shipped

Implemented and verified. Available today by building from main — there is no release yet, so that is the only way to get any of it.

ItemStatusWhat was verified
Native on Windows, macOS, LinuxDONEOne source tree, three native builds. Fast3D over OpenGL via SDL2. No emulator, no recompiler, no compatibility layer.
All 27 stages bootDONEEvery loadable level renders and exits without a crash or hang — measured, not assumed.
Split-screen multiplayerDONERadar, all 64 characters, all five watch pages, saves persist.
Rulesets & horde modeDONEClassic / hardcore / survival / chaos / horde plus nine individual percentages. Measured on Agent: hardcore takes AI health 2.000 → 1.000 and ammo 2.000 → 1.000.
FOV systemDONEfov 50–160, applied at the perspective call. Two earlier attempts scaled the wrong thing and moved triangle counts convincingly, which is why this one names its verification.
Filtering, mipmaps, MSAA, anisotropicDONEThree-point filtering is the real N64 behaviour and the default. Anisotropic is clamped to the driver maximum; MSAA verified at 4 samples.
Lua moddingDONEA folder with a mod.lua in it, loaded at startup, no rebuild. Read API into live game state. The CRT screen ships as a readable example mod.
Metal renderer backendDONELanded 2026-08-26 on macOS — boots to the title crawl. Three-point filtering and depth-tested blob shadows followed. Unlocks tvOS/iOS work.
The launcherDONEEight pages over the existing setting surface. Every control resolves to a gate that already worked from a shell.
Mouse lookDONEModern mouse look with a classic fallback, sensitivity and inversion, and a fix for fast camera movement. Note: docs/VISION.md item 5 still scores this OPEN — that row is stale, and the shipped work is in the changelog.
Developer console & toolingDONETyped core, cross-renderer UI, a game-thread-owned pause policy, read-only session queries, explicit-slot mutations and controlled mission transitions. Launcher Developer Tools page and local telemetry reports.
Enemy gibs & Brutal GoldenEyeDONEPolicy-gated gibs, textured blood, a Base Game switch that suppresses the whole family without discarding your preferences.

Partial

Real code that does something real, with a gap that is stated rather than glossed. Details for each are on Known bugs & limitations.

ItemStatusThe gap
High frame rate above 60PARTIALThe most consequential open problem in the project, in its own words. Gameplay is tick-coupled, so rendering faster runs the world faster. A fixed simulation tick with interpolated rendering is the correct fix.
Resolution / aspectPARTIALResolution, aspect, fullscreen and supersampling work. A wider window fits the 4:3 view rather than widening the horizontal field, and split-screen's per-viewport aspect has not been audited against an arbitrary host window.
Co-op campaignPARTIALPlayers spawn, separate and render, and movement through the player API was verified over 6,001 frames against a no-input control. Objectives, AI and cutscenes still assume one Bond, and two humans at two pads is untested.
Modern controller supportPARTIALRare's eight styles, glyph profiles, per-player bindings and deadzone all exist. A modern stick-response option alongside Classic N64 is #62; complete independent keyboard/mouse/gamepad rebinding is #63.
Multiplayer botsPARTIALMultiplayer itself works. Bots do not exist yet; the offline navmesh, prop-extent and node-table work behind them is done.
Custom missions & scriptingPARTIALThe scripting host exists. Mission authoring does not.
HD texture replacementPARTIALA hash-keyed pack lookup is implemented but unverified — no compiler was available to check the path. Off by default.
tvOS (Apple TV)PARTIALBring-up, not a product. Builds, signs and deploys to real hardware, on GL ES or Metal.
iOSPARTIALBring-up. lib and app build against Metal and there is an iOS Simulator build, which needs no paired hardware; deploying to a device does. iOS work started after Metal was already the proven renderer.
AndroidPARTIALBring-up. The port layer builds for arm64 GL ES and carries on-screen touch controls as a virtual pad. It has never been run, so it is not yet a running game.

Disputed

Where the project's own documents disagree with each other. Both are kept on purpose — recording a real disagreement is more useful than declaring a winner prematurely.

Lockstep netplay DISPUTED

for docs/NETPLAY.md against docs/PLAYER_API.md reviewed 2026-09-09

A lockstep session exists in the tree and is alpha. NETPLAY.md argues the model can work; PLAYER_API.md argues against it from measurements. Netplay determinism itself was verified over 3,000 identical frames, so the disagreement is about the model rather than about determinism. Online play is downstream of the frame-timing work either way.

Whether co-op players move RESOLVED, DOCS STALE

current docs/ROADMAP.md §M4 stale docs/VISION.md item 38, docs/FAQ.md reviewed 2026-09-09

Resolved in favour of "they move": the earlier conclusion came from an accessor that returned a field zeroed at spawn and rarely written, so both players were walking while the measurement said otherwise. The bug was in the measuring tool. Two documents still carry the old conclusion and have not been updated; that is recorded here rather than fixed silently on a website.

Blocked

Not merely unstarted — waiting on something specific and named.

ItemBlocked on
Online playThe fixed-tick work. Rollback or interpolation over a variable tick is not worth attempting, so this waits on the frame-timing problem above.
ADS, manual reload, dual wield, secondary fireAll tick-coupled, so all downstream of the same frame-timing problem.
Third-person camera, animation, collisionThe camera/player orientation split, which has not been done.
An in-game options menuAn explicit maintainer comment not to change the Watch's screen-count constant until the player struct is fully shiftable. This is why the launcher exists instead, and why only three keys are live.
Any official release or downloadEight readiness gates in docs/RELEASING.md, none met: coordinated macOS packaging, clean-machine Windows acceptance, a licensing and code-signing decision, and a reviewed permission boundary among them. See Releases & versions.
Distributing a playable binaryUnresolvable as stated: a built binary contains the entire game. It is not a scheduling problem and it is not on this roadmap.

Planned

Not started. Listed because the shape is understood, not because a date exists — there are no dates on this page and there deliberately are none.

ItemStatusNote
True aspect-aware widescreenOPENThe distinction that matters is whether widescreen widens the horizontal field or stretches a 4:3 image; that has not been measured.
Free camera / photo modeOPENA fly camera exists behind GETV_FREECAM and is beta: visibility stays rooted at Bond's room, so flying out of it culls the world. Re-rooting the portal walk was tried and deliberately not shipped because it could never be shown to run.
Dynamic lighting, shadows, post-processingOPENThe config keys exist and are inert. See the reserved list.
Night vision, thermal, remote cameraOPENPost-processing over an existing frame, and the cheapest items in their range.
Enhanced guard AI, communication, personalitiesOPENDownstream of the bot work.
Custom levelsOPENThe hardest item on the list: it needs the setup / STAN / background pipeline running in reverse.
Custom weapons and enemiesOPENData-table driven, and considerably more tractable than levels.
Randomizer, weather, environmental audioOPENNot started.
VROPENA camera seam exists for a per-eye transform. Listed because the hook exists, not because it works.
